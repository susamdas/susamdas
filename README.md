## 🔭 Featured Project: FinLens

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://github.com/susamdas/finlens">
    <img src="https://github.com/susamdas/finlens/raw/HEAD/docs/screenshots/overview.png" alt="FinLens overview dashboard" width="90%" />
  </a>
</p>

**[FinLens](https://github.com/susamdas/finlens)** is a global financial inclusion intelligence platform built on the **World Bank Global Findex Database 2025**. Roughly 1.3 billion adults still have no account, and the Findex data that tracks them is published as a 430-series spreadsheet. FinLens turns it into an interactive tool that shows **who is left out, where, and how that is changing**.

- 🌍 **162 economies, 5 survey waves (2011–2024)**, with breakdowns for 13 population groups
- 📊 **23 pages**: global map, country profiles, side-by-side comparisons, inclusion gaps by gender and income, digital finance, trends, and forecasts to 2030
- 🏦 **Microfinance Lens**: who saves, who borrows, and who is excluded
- 🤖 **AI Analyst** that answers only from the data and cites its sources
- 🔒 **Private by design**: runs fully in the browser, no accounts, no tracking
- ✅ **137 tests**, end-to-end checks on every page, and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility checks
